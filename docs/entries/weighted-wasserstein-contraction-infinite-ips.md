# Weighted Wasserstein contraction for infinite particle systems

## Criterion

Let \(V\) be countable and let configurations \(\eta=(\eta(x))_{x\in V}\) have nonnegative continuous spins. Choose positive weights \(v(x)\) and the tempered state space
\[
\mathcal X=\left\{\eta:\|\eta\|_v:=\sum_{x\in V}v(x)\eta(x)<\infty\right\}.
\]
Equip probability laws with finite first moment with the Wasserstein distance induced by \(\|\cdot\|_v\).

Bezborodov--Di Persio--Friesen--Kuchling consider a broad interacting jump-diffusion/branching system on this space. Under their structural assumptions, Theorem 1.1 gives a pathwise comparison principle: ordered initial configurations, driven by common noises, remain ordered. If the effective drift satisfies, for some \(A>0\) and every ordered pair \(\xi\le\eta\),
\[
\sum_{x\in V}v(x)\bigl(\widetilde B(x,\eta)-\widetilde B(x,\xi)\bigr)
\le -A\|\eta-\xi\|_v,
\]
then Theorem 1.2 gives
\[
W_1(P_t^*\rho,P_t^*\widetilde\rho)
\le e^{-At}W_1(\rho,\widetilde\rho).
\]
In particular there is a unique invariant law in the finite-first-moment class and exponential convergence to it in \(W_1\).

## Mechanism

The distance is adapted to an infinite system rather than being an unweighted finite-volume Hamming distance. The weights make configurations with infinitely many nonzero coordinates admissible while controlling their influence at spatial infinity.

For ordered deterministic starts, the comparison coupling gives \(\xi_t\le\eta_t\). Positivity removes the absolute value coordinate by coordinate, so the expected weighted distance is the weighted difference of first moments. The effective-drift inequality gives a Gronwall contraction. Section 7 extends the estimate from ordered deterministic pairs to arbitrary initial laws by inserting ordered envelopes and optimizing over initial couplings.

Thus the proof interface is a contractive weighted transportation metric generated by common stochastic forcing. It does not require contraction of every individual update, a finite configuration space, or a shortest-path extension from neighboring configurations as in finite-state [path coupling](path-coupling-glauber-dynamics.md).

## Representative IPS use

The paper's class includes infinite-type continuous-state branching processes with immigration, interacting branching systems and population models on countable graphs. Example 2.3 treats an infinite-type continuous-state branching process with immigration. When weighted interaction/migration coefficients are dominated by the local dissipative term by a positive margin, Theorem 1.2 applies and the infinite-dimensional process converges to its unique invariant distribution.

## Limitations

The theorem is a subcritical strong-dissipativity criterion. The effective drift must contract the weighted total discrepancy uniformly by a positive rate; this can fail near criticality even when uniqueness remains true. The comparison principle also uses monotonicity and the positive cone. Finally, convergence is in \(W_1\) on the finite-first-moment class, not automatically in total variation and not automatically a spectral-gap or entropy statement.
