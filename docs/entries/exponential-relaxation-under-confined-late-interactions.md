# Exponential relaxation under confined late interactions

The modified spin system from [undoing duality under confined interactions](undoing-duality-under-confined-interactions.md) is the product of a zero-boundary process on \(R\) and independent two-state chains on \(R^c\). Local approximation and exponential relaxation of these components give exponential convergence of a patch term whose late successful interactions are confined to \(R\).

## Assumptions

Assume that the original spin system is uniformly bounded and finite range, and that \(\Lambda\) has [polynomial growth](polynomial-growth-lattice.md).

For \(R\Subset\Lambda\), let \(P_t^{R,0}\) be the semigroup of the zero-boundary restriction of the original spin system to \(R\). Assume that there is \(\gamma_1>0\), independent of \(R\), such that for every \(R\Subset\Lambda\), the restricted system has a unique invariant measure \(\nu_R\) and there is \(C_R^0<\infty\) for which

$$
\sup_{\eta\in\{0,1\}^R}
\left|
P_t^{R,0}f(\eta)-\nu_R(f)
\right|
\le
C_R^0e^{-\gamma_1t}\|f\|_\infty
$$

for every bounded \(f\colon\{0,1\}^R\to\mathbb R\).

For \(i\notin R\), the exterior spin is a two-state chain with jump rates \(c_i^0(\mathbf0)\) from \(0\) to \(1\) and \(c_i^1(\mathbf0)\) from \(1\) to \(0\). Its exponential ergodicity rate is

$$
c_i^0(\mathbf0)+c_i^1(\mathbf0).
$$

Set

$$
\gamma_2
=
\inf_{i\in\Lambda}
\left(
c_i^0(\mathbf0)+c_i^1(\mathbf0)
\right),
\qquad
\gamma
=
\min(\gamma_1,\gamma_2),
$$

and assume \(\gamma_2>0\). The modified generator \(\cL_R\) has invariant measure

$$
\mu_R
=
\nu_R
\otimes
\bigotimes_{i\notin R}
\operatorname{Ber}
\left(
\frac{c_i^0(\mathbf0)}
{c_i^0(\mathbf0)+c_i^1(\mathbf0)}
\right).
$$

## Lemma

For every \(A\Subset\Lambda\), \(T<\infty\), \(R\Subset\Lambda\), and \(0<\gamma'<\gamma\), there is \(C_{A,R,T,\gamma'}<\infty\) such that, for every \(t\ge T\),

$$
\sup_{\xi\in\{0,1\}^\Lambda}
\left|
\mathbb E_A
\left[
W_t^\xi\ind(E_{T,t}^R)
\right]
-
\mu_R\left(P_T(\chi_A)\right)
\right|
\le
C_{A,R,T,\gamma'}e^{-\gamma'(t-T)}.
$$

In particular, the limit is the same for every initial configuration \(\xi\).

## Proof

The process generated by \(\cL_R\) evolves independently on \(R\) and at each site of \(R^c\). If \(h\) is supported on \(Q\Subset\Lambda\), the assumed bound on the process in \(R\) and the product coupling of the exterior chains give

$$
\left\|P_s^Rh-\mu_R(h)\right\|_\infty
\le
\left(C_R^0+2|Q\setminus R|\right)e^{-\gamma s}\|h\|_\infty.
\tag{1}
$$

Set \(g=P_T\chi_A\). For \(w>0\) and \(s\ge0\), let

$$
Q_s=B(A,w(1+s)),
\qquad
g_s=P_T^{Q_s,0}\chi_A.
$$

The graphical estimate in [finite propagation for zero-boundary restrictions](finite-propagation-for-zero-boundary-restrictions.md) applies with the evolution time fixed at \(T\) and the boundary distance proportional to \(1+s\). By taking \(w\) sufficiently large, for any prescribed \(a>0\) there is \(C_{A,T,a}<\infty\) such that

$$
\|g-g_s\|_\infty\le C_{A,T,a}e^{-as}.
\tag{2}
$$

The function \(g_s\) is supported on \(Q_s\), satisfies \(\|g_s\|_\infty\le1\), and polynomial growth gives \(|Q_s|\le C_{A,T,w}(1+s)^D\). Equations (1) and (2) therefore imply

$$
\left\|P_s^Rg-\mu_R(g)\right\|_\infty
\le
2C_{A,T,a}e^{-as}
+
\left(C_R^0+2C_{A,T,w}(1+s)^D\right)e^{-\gamma s}.
$$

Choose \(a>\gamma'\) and absorb the polynomial factor into the difference between \(\gamma\) and \(\gamma'\). Finally, the confined-interaction identity gives

$$
\mathbb E_A\left[W_t^\xi\ind(E_{T,t}^R)\right]
=
\left(P_{t-T}^RP_T\chi_A\right)(\xi),
$$

which proves the lemma with \(s=t-T\).
