\section{Finite Hessian patches and a deterministic auxiliary result}
\label{sec:patches}

We now specialize to the quadratic Hessian equation~\eqref{eq:hessian-forward}. The first result is finite and algebraic. It changes the unit at which later absolute values are taken without changing the signed Picard expansion.

Define the bilinear operator
\begin{equation*}
  \cB(f,g)(t)
  =
  \lambda\int_0^t
  \partial_x^2P_{t-s}[f(s)g(s)]\,ds.
\end{equation*}
Set
\begin{equation*}
  z^{[0]}=L,
  \qquad
  z^{[N+1]}=L+\cB(z^{[N]},z^{[N]}).
\end{equation*}
Expanding algebraically gives a finite sum over rooted planar binary trees of height at most $N$.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-depth patch regrouping]
\label{thm:finite-patch-regrouping}
For every integer $N\geq0$, the finite expansion of $z^{[N]}$ may be reorganized uniquely by its decorated patch skeleton. Replacing each maximal left-spine chain by its complete contribution~\eqref{eq:complete-patch} gives exactly the same signed function $z^{[N]}$.

In particular, composing all Hessian transfers inside a patch before taking an absolute value is an exact finite-level operation. No term is discarded or added, and no convergence of an infinite tree series is used.
\end{theorem}

The next theorem is deterministic and weaker than the representation results which follow. Its purpose is to identify a small classical solution by a direct PDE iteration and to provide an overlapping uniqueness regime.

Fix $0<\alpha<1$. Let $\mathcal A_{\alpha,T}$ be the coefficients $a\in C^{\alpha/2,\alpha}([0,T]\times\Torus)$ satisfying
\begin{equation*}
  \frac38\leq a\leq\frac58,
  \qquad
  [a]_{C^{\alpha/2,\alpha}}\leq\frac18.
\end{equation*}
Let $C_{\mathrm{Sch}}(\alpha,T)$ be a uniform Schauder constant over this class, so that the solution of
\begin{equation*}
  \partial_tv=av_{xx},
  \qquad v(0)=\phi,
\end{equation*}
satisfies
\begin{equation*}
  \norm{v_{xx}}_{C^{\alpha/2,\alpha}}
  \leq
  C_{\mathrm{Sch}}(\alpha,T)\norm\phi_{C^{2+\alpha}}.
\end{equation*}
Put
\begin{equation*}
  R=C_{\mathrm{Sch}}(\alpha,T)\norm\phi_{C^{2+\alpha}}.
\end{equation*}

\begin{theorem}[Self-consistent deterministic iteration]
\label{thm:deterministic-iteration}
Assume
\begin{equation}
  \abs\lambda R\leq\frac18.
  \label{eq:deterministic-smallness}
\end{equation}
Set $z_0=\phi''$. Given $z_n$, let $v_{n+1}$ solve
\begin{equation*}
  \partial_tv_{n+1}
  =
  \pa{\frac12+\lambda z_n}v_{n+1,xx},
  \qquad
  v_{n+1}(0)=\phi,
\end{equation*}
and put $z_{n+1}=v_{n+1,xx}$. Then the iterates remain in the H\"older ball of radius $R$, the coefficients remain in $[3/8,5/8]$, and $(z_n)$ converges in $L^2$ and uniformly to $z=v_{xx}$. The limit $v$ is a classical solution of~\eqref{eq:hessian-forward} and is unique among solutions with $\abs{\lambda v_{xx}}\leq1/8$.

For each observation time $t\leq T$,
\begin{equation*}
  v(t,x)=\E_x[\phi(X_t)],
\end{equation*}
where
\begin{equation*}
  dX_s
  =
  \sqrt{1+2\lambda z(t-s,X_s)}\,dW_s,
  \qquad
  X_0=x.
\end{equation*}
The diffusion representation is implicit because its coefficient contains the fixed point $z$.
\end{theorem}
