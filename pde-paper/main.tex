\begin{abstract}
Branching representations for nonlinear parabolic equations are often exact before absolute values are taken and nonintegrable afterward. We study this distinction for representations with derivative weights. First, for the coding-tree construction of Nguwi, Penent, and Privault, we isolate a repeated-Hessian genealogy which forces failure of $L^1$ whenever an even terminal jet grows faster than the ordered-simplex scale. Conversely, integrability of the relevant code-rooted trees imposes a Gevrey-$1/2$ bound on the directional jet. A benchmark equation then shows that this failure is representation-dependent: the coding-tree functional is non-$L^1$ at every positive horizon while a marked branching construction of Henry-Labord\`ere, Oudjane, Tan, Touzi, and Warin is $L^2$ for short time.

The main results concern
\begin{equation*}
  \partial_t v=\frac12v_{xx}+\lambda(v_{xx})^2
\end{equation*}
on the one-dimensional torus. Finite Duhamel trees admit an exact regrouping into maximal Hessian patches. Averaging the continuous variables inside each decorated skeleton before taking absolute values yields, under an explicit small-data condition, an absolutely convergent skeleton expansion and an unbiased $L^1$ estimator. In contrast, for one fixed arbitrarily small smooth datum, every raw-faithful estimator has infinite first moment. Raw-faithfulness requires the canonical raw signed marked integrand to remain the conditional barycenter after the raw marks are exposed; the obstruction is invariant under lifetime, genealogy, and Gaussian proposal changes.

The coarsening class is nevertheless nontrivial. Naive patchwise Gaussian-bridge coarsening still fails on the obstruction combs because every maximal-left patch there has one edge. By contrast, retaining the actual branch times on one canonical maximal-left spine while averaging all other continuous marks gives an unbiased $L^1$ representation under one additional geometric smallness condition. Thus the proved hierarchy contains a nonintegrable raw-faithful level, an integrable representation with genuine time randomness, and the fully interior-averaged C-prime representation.
\end{abstract}
