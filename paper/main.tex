\ABSTRACT{%
{\color{red} Instead of calm and facilitating say that we're extending the class of KCSMs naturally. They are not nice in the usual sense, no reversibility, no attractivity, but we show they have a version of monotonicity. Need examples, maybe with pictures of trajectories to show how they behave very similarly on macro scale. Main objective to introduce new class that is joined by a form of monotonicity (check if patch positivity sharply defines the class with this order). Say monotone isntead of attractive. "c.m. monotone". Check sharpness of this monotonicity class and also for the p<0.5 condition for convergence thm. }
We consider spin systems with a facilitating state, which can cause, permit,
or accelerate neighbouring updates, and another state that is referred to as calm. We
introduce a patch representation which averages consecutive local updates
before their signed contributions are compared. {\color{red} completly unclear what means signed contributions.}
Starting from the signed monomial Feynman--Kac dual, we retain only interactions
with nonempty target that act on the dual process. Conditional on this skeleton,
the marked interactions in the resulting one-site spacetime patches are
independent and give an exact representation of the semigroup. We characterize
nonnegative patch contributions by inequalities for the multilinear
coefficients of the two flip rates. This patch positivity implies preservation
of an order on moments centered at a calm-state density profile and a
comparison with the dynamics obtained by removing independent creation of
facilitating states. On interaction graphs of polynomial growth, uniform
independent creation of facilitating states yields convergence, at rate
$O((1+t)^D e^{-\varepsilon t/2})$, from a class~$\mathcal M_-$ defined by
centered moments to a common invariant law. When the threshold profile is at
most~$1/2$, convergence is uniform over all initial configurations. FA-1f and
the biased annihilating branching process illustrate the criterion.}
