\ABSTRACT{%
Kinetically constrained spin models have monotone constraints, but their dynamics need not be monotone in the usual configuration order. We introduce patch-positive spin systems, a class of spin systems for which this lost monotonicity is recovered at the level of probability laws. More precisely, we show that the semigroup preserves an order defined by centered moments. This yields comparison results between non-monotone dynamics, including systems that differ by environment-independent transitions from~$1$ to~$0$.
On interaction graphs of polynomial growth, a uniform positive rate of such transitions implies convergence from a broad class of initial laws to a common invariant measure. If the patch threshold profile is at most~$1/2$ the system is uniformly exponentially ergodic.
The proof is based on a graphical representation obtained from a signed Feynman--Kac dual by averaging local randomness over spacetime patches. FA-1f, biased annihilating branching process and the contact process are patch-positive examples.}
