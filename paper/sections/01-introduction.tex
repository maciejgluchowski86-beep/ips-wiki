\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

\begin{discussion}
  \item Introduce the problem: ordinary monomial duality exists for a
  broad class of spin systems, but its signs obstruct direct positive
  pathwise arguments.
  \item Explain at a conceptual level why conditioning on successful
  interactions produces one-site spacetime patches.
  \item Explain what is gained by averaging inside patches rather than
  at individual dual transitions.
  \item Make explicit that the framework is not spatially one-sided:
  no orientation assumption occurs in
  \cref{thm:patch-factorization,thm:patch-representation,thm:patch-positivity-criterion,thm:centered-order,thm:pure-death-comparison}.
  \item Say that a uniform pure-death component is required only for
  \cref{thm:common-limit}.
\end{discussion}

\subsection{Main results}

\begin{discussion}
  \item Present \cref{thm:patch-factorization,thm:patch-representation}
  as the conceptual core: conditional independence of patches and the
  exact bulk--end random product.
  \item Present \cref{thm:patch-positivity-criterion,prop:critical-density-formula}
  as the local algebraic criterion and the formula for the representation
  threshold \pstar.
  \item Present \cref{thm:centered-order} as the preservation theorem
  for \Mstar. Explain that the generator proof and the patch-product
  proof are two distinct uses of the same coefficient condition.
  \item Present \cref{thm:pure-death-comparison} as the principal
  comparison consequence of the positive patch product.
  \item Present \cref{thm:common-limit} as the main dynamical
  consequence under the additional uniform pure-death hypothesis.
  Link any use of \ergodicity\ or \uniformexponentialergodicity\ to
  \cref{def:ergodicity,def:uniform-exponential-ergodicity}.
  \item Explain that \pstar\ is a representation threshold, not a
  thermodynamic critical density.
\end{discussion}

\subsection{Relation to the literature}

  \item Locate signed monomial duality within general Markov duality and

  \item Separate established ingredients from the proposed
  contributions: successful-interaction conditioning, finite-horizon
  patch factorization, the positive patch product, the local positivity
  criterion, centered-moment preservation, pure-death comparison, and
  the common-limit theorem.
  \item State only novelty claims that will be retained after a dedicated
  literature review. Do not use this paragraph to survey FA-1f or BABP.
\end{discussion}
