\section{Patch positivity and centered-moment order}
\label{sec:patch-positivity}

Section~\ref{sec:representation} expresses the signed dual weight as a product
of averaged patch contributions. Patch positivity requires every bulk
contribution~$C(P)$ to be nonnegative, but its definition quantifies over all
possible bulk patches. We show that this condition is equivalent to explicit
inequalities on the local coefficients of the flip rates, and then determine
the threshold~$p_i^\star$ above which every end contribution based at~$i$ is
nonnegative. These sign properties turn the patch representation into
comparisons of moments, while a centered-monomial calculation gives the
stronger preservation of the centered-moment order. We also use the same
structure to compare dynamics that differ by pure deaths and to transfer
convergence between ordered initial laws.
