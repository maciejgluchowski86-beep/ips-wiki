\section{Patch positivity and centered-moment order}
\label{sec:patch-positivity}

Theorem~\ref{thm:patch-representation} gives the representation
\begin{equation*}
(\mu P_t)(\chi_A)
=
\E_A\Cb{
\prod_{P\in\mathcal B_t}C(P)\,
\mu\bb{
\prod_{P\in\mathcal E_t}C\bb{\eta\bb{i(P)},P}
}
}.
\end{equation*}
The patch contributions appearing here are defined in
Section~\ref{sec:representation}, where the representation is proved. To obtain
inequalities from this formula, we need to control the sign of patch contributions. We call the
system patch-positive when~$C(P)\geq0$ for every bulk patch~$P$. We show that this condition is
equivalent to explicit inequalities on the coefficients of the flip rates.
Bulk positivity does not determine the sign of an end contribution, since
$C(z,P)$ still depends on its terminal argument~$z$. For each site~$i$, we
therefore determine the smallest~$p_i\in[0,1]$ for which
$C(p,P)\geq0$ for every end patch based at~$i$ and call this value the patch
threshold~$p_i^\star$. These two sign controls are what allow the patch
representation to yield the moment comparisons proved later in the section.
