% BLURB A. Why conditioning on the skeleton helps
%
% Reader question:
%   We have fixed the successful-interaction skeleton. What information is still
%   hidden, and why is it useful that this remaining randomness is local?
%
% Job:
%   Say first what G_T reveals and what it does not. It fixes the patch
%   decomposition and all successful-interaction records up to time T. The

%   the resulting one-site history must have exactly the boundaries prescribed

Conditioning on~$\mathcal G_T$ fixes the patch decomposition and all
successful-interaction records up to time~$T$, but it does not reveal the kind of a recorded
interaction and deaths between successive successful interactions. These hidden variables must however be compatible with the fixed
patch boundaries. Depending on the boundary types, this may determine an
interaction kind or rule out some deaths. The key point is that these
constraints are local to individual patches. Conditional on~$\mathcal G_T$,
the remaining randomness factorizes over patches and can be explicitly averaged. This averaging retains cancellations
between different histories with the same successful-interaction skeleton. {\color{red} Abnormal language everywhere. "histories" wtf. Also again you talk of cancellations but the reader doesnt know where or what.}
