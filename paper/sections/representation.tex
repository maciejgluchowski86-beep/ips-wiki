% BLURB A. Why conditioning on the skeleton helps
%
% Reader question:
%   We have fixed the successful-interaction skeleton. What information is still
%   unresolved, and what does its locality buy us?
%
% Job:
%   Say first what G_T fixes and what it leaves unresolved. It fixes all
%   successful interactions and hence the patch decomposition up to time T. It
%   does not reveal the local information deliberately omitted from the
%   skeleton. In particular, deaths inside patches are not recorded, and for a
%   recorded outgoing interaction the skeleton does not say whether it was a
%   split or a birth.
%
%   The fixed patch boundaries restrict this remaining randomness. Depending on
%   the boundary types, they may force an interaction kind or exclude a death.

% Keep out:
%   Sigma_P, alpha(P), Con(P), averaging, cancellations, and the formal

%   precise; Blurbs E--F explain what is averaged and why.
% ============================================================================

Conditioning on~$\mathcal G_T$ fixes the successful interactions and hence the
patch decomposition up to time~$T$. It leaves unresolved the local information
that was omitted from the skeleton. In particular, deaths inside patches are
not recorded, and for a recorded outgoing interaction the skeleton does not say
whether it was a split or a birth. The fixed patch boundaries restrict this
remaining randomness: depending on the boundary types, they may force the
interaction kind or exclude a death. These restrictions involve only one patch
at a time. Consequently, conditional on~$\mathcal G_T$, the unresolved
randomness factorizes over the patches.
