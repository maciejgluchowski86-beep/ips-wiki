\subsection{Centered-moment order}
\label{sec:moment-order}
\label{subsec:moment-monotonicity}

Part~\textup{(ii)} of Theorem~\ref{thm:patch-positivity-order} can be proven
directly from the generator in the centered-monomial basis. {\color{red} Reader would be lost. It should be stated what our aim in the proof is, to show that the generator expansion has non-negative off diagonal terms and why this suffices} If~$i\notin A$,
the update at~$i$ leaves~$\chi_A^*$ unchanged. If~$i\in A$, then
\begin{equation*}
\lambda_i\bb{E_{i,\rho_i}-I}\chi_A^*
=
\lambda_i\bb{\rho_i-p_i^\star}\chi_{A\setminus\cb{i}}^*
-
\lambda_i\chi_A^*.
\end{equation*}
Expanding~$\lambda_i\rho_i$ and~$\lambda_i$ in ordinary monomials and summing
over~$i\in A$ gives
\begin{align*}
\Lcal\chi_A^*
=\sum_{i\in A}\Bigg\{
&-\lambda_i(\mb 0)\chi_A^*
+
\sum_{S\subseteq N(i)}
\bb{
\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(S)
-p_i^\star\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)
}
\chi_S\chi_{A\setminus\cb{i}}^*+
\sum_{\substack{S\subseteq N(i)\\S\neq\vn}}
\bb{-\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)}
\chi_S\chi_A^*
\Bigg\}.
\end{align*}
Thus to prove preservation of the centered-moment order it suffices to verify the above
coefficients of~$\chi_S\chi_B^*$ terms are non-negative and that the terms themselves have non-negative coefficient expansion in
centered-monomial basis.

Part~\textup{(i)} gives exactly the required signs. For every nonempty
$S\subseteq N(i)$,
\begin{equation*}
-\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)\geq0.
\end{equation*}
For the other coefficient consider~$S=\vn$. Since
$p_i^\star\leq\rho_i(\mb 0)$,
\begin{equation*}
\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(\vn)
-p_i^\star\widehat{\lambda_i}(\vn)
=
\lambda_i(\mb 0)\bb{\rho_i(\mb 0)-p_i^\star}
\geq0.
\end{equation*}
If~$S\neq\vn$ and~$\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)<0$, the definition of
$p_i^\star$ gives
\begin{equation*}
\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(S)
-p_i^\star\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)
\geq0.
\end{equation*}
If instead~$\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)=0$, the second coefficient inequality in
part~\textup{(i)} gives~$\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(S)\geq0$. Hence every
coefficient multiplying~$\chi_S\chi_{A\setminus\cb{i}}^*$ or
$\chi_S\chi_A^*$ above is nonnegative.

It remains only to express these products in the centered basis. At each site,
\begin{equation*}
\eta(j)=p_j^\star+\bb{\eta(j)-p_j^\star},
\qquad
\eta(j)\bb{\eta(j)-p_j^\star}
=\bb{1-p_j^\star}\eta(j).
\end{equation*}
Since~$0\leq p_j^\star\leq1$, repeated use of these identities shows that
$\chi_S\chi_B^*$ is a nonnegative linear combination of centered monomials for
every finite~$S,B$ {\color{red} This should be shown.}. Thus, apart from the diagonal term
$-\lambda_i(\mb 0)\chi_A^*$, the generator sends each centered monomial to a
nonnegative linear combination of centered monomials.
