\subsection{Centered-moment order}
\label{sec:moment-order}
\label{subsec:moment-monotonicity}

We now prove part~\textup{(ii)} of Theorem~\ref{thm:patch-positivity-order}.
We write the generator in the centered-monomial basis and show that its
off-diagonal coefficients are nonnegative. The corresponding equations for the
centered moments then form a row-finite Metzler system, so the standard
positivity theorem for Metzler generators~\cite{ArendtEtAl1986} gives
preservation of nonnegative centered moments.

If~$i\notin A$, the update at~$i$ leaves~$\chi_A^*$ unchanged. If~$i\in A$,
then
\begin{equation*}
\lambda_i\bb{E_{i,\rho_i}-I}\chi_A^*
=
\lambda_i\bb{\rho_i-p_i^\star}\chi_{A\setminus\cb{i}}^*
-
\lambda_i\chi_A^*.
\end{equation*}
Expanding~$\lambda_i\rho_i$ and~$\lambda_i$ in monomials and summing over
$i\in A$ gives
\begin{equation*}
\Lcal\chi_A^*
=\sum_{i\in A}\Cb{
-\lambda_i(\mb 0)\chi_A^*
+
\sum_{S\subseteq N(i)}
\bb{
\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(S)
-p_i^\star\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)
}
\chi_S\chi_{A\setminus\cb{i}}^*
+
\sum_{\substack{S\subseteq N(i)\\S\neq\vn}}
\bb{-\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)}
\chi_S\chi_A^*
}.
\end{equation*}
Part~\textup{(i)} gives
\begin{equation*}
-\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)\geq0
\quad\text{for }S\neq\vn,
\qquad
\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(S)
-p_i^\star\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)
\geq0
\quad\text{for every }S\subseteq N(i).
\end{equation*}
For the second inequality, the case~$S=\vn$ is
$\lambda_i(\mb 0)(\rho_i(\mb 0)-p_i^\star)\geq0$. If~$S\neq\vn$ and
$\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)<0$, it follows from the definition of~$p_i^\star$; if
$\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)=0$, it follows from the second coefficient inequality
in part~\textup{(i)}.

It remains to expand the products~$\chi_S\chi_B^*$ in the centered basis. For
finite~$S,B\subseteq\Lambda$,
\begin{equation*}
\chi_S\chi_B^*
=
\bb{\prod_{j\in S\cap B}\bb{1-p_j^\star}}
\sum_{R\subseteq S}
\bb{\prod_{j\in S\setminus R}p_j^\star}
\chi_{R\cup(B\setminus S)}^*.
\end{equation*}
Indeed, first use
$\eta(j)(\eta(j)-p_j^\star)=(1-p_j^\star)\eta(j)$ on~$S\cap B$, and then
expand~$\eta(j)=p_j^\star+(\eta(j)-p_j^\star)$ for~$j\in S$. Since
$0\leq p_j^\star\leq1$, every coefficient is nonnegative. Hence, after
collecting equal centered monomials,
\begin{equation*}
\Lcal\chi_A^*
=
\sum_{B\Subset\Lambda}M(A,B)\chi_B^*,
\qquad
M(A,B)\geq0\quad\text{for }B\neq A,
\end{equation*}
and for each~$A$ only finitely many coefficients~$M(A,B)$ are nonzero.

Let~$\sigma$ be a finite signed measure and put
$m_A(t)=(\sigma P_t)(\chi_A^*)$. The backward equation gives
\begin{equation*}
\frac{d}{dt}m_A(t)
=
\sum_{B\Subset\Lambda}M(A,B)m_B(t).
\end{equation*}
This is a row-finite Metzler system. By the positivity result cited above,
$m_A(0)\geq0$ for every finite~$A$ implies~$m_A(t)\geq0$ for every finite~$A$
and~$t\geq0$. Taking~$\sigma=\nu-\mu$ therefore proves
\begin{equation*}
\mu\preceq_*\nu
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mu P_t\preceq_*\nu P_t
\qquad\text{for every }t\geq0.
\end{equation*}

The same argument with~$\sigma=\mu$ shows that~$\mathcal M_*$ is preserved by
the semigroup. Ordinary monomial comparison follows from expansion
\begin{equation*}
\chi_A
=
\sum_{B\subseteq A}
\bb{\prod_{i\in A\setminus B}p_i^\star}\chi_B^*,
\end{equation*}
whose coefficients are nonnegative. Finally, every nonempty centered moment of
$\mu_{\mb p^\star}$ is zero, so
$\mu_{\mb p^\star}\preceq_*\mu$ for every~$\mu\in\mathcal M_*$. Applying the
monomial comparison gives
\begin{equation*}
(\mu_{\mb p^\star}P_t)(\chi_A)
\leq
(\mu P_t)(\chi_A).
\end{equation*}
This proves part~\textup{(ii)} and completes the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:patch-positivity-order}.
