\subsection{Centered-moment order}
\label{sec:moment-order}
\label{subsec:moment-monotonicity}

We now prove part~\textup{(ii)} of Theorem~\ref{thm:patch-positivity-order}.
The aim is to show that, in the centered-monomial basis, the generator has
nonnegative off-diagonal coefficients. In finite volume this is enough: the
semigroup matrix is the exponential of the generator matrix, so it has
nonnegative entries and sends every centered monomial to a nonnegative linear
combination of centered monomials. If~$\mu\preceq_*\nu$, the signed measure
$\nu-\mu$ is nonnegative on every centered monomial, and hence remains so after
the semigroup is applied. We therefore first establish the coefficient signs
and then pass from finite zero-boundary volumes to~$\Lambda$.

The generator calculation is local. If~$i\notin A$, the update at~$i$ leaves
$\chi_A^*$ unchanged. If~$i\in A$, then
\begin{equation*}
\lambda_i\bb{E_{i,\rho_i}-I}\chi_A^*
=
\lambda_i\bb{\rho_i-p_i^\star}\chi_{A\setminus\cb{i}}^*
-
\lambda_i\chi_A^*.
\end{equation*}
Expanding~$\lambda_i\rho_i$ and~$\lambda_i$ in ordinary monomials and summing
over~$i\in A$ gives
\begin{align*}
\Lcal\chi_A^*
=\sum_{i\in A}\Bigg\{
&-\lambda_i(\mb 0)\chi_A^*
+
\sum_{S\subseteq N(i)}
\bb{
\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(S)
-p_i^\star\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)
}
\chi_S\chi_{A\setminus\cb{i}}^*
\\
&+
\sum_{\substack{S\subseteq N(i)\\S\neq\vn}}
\bb{-\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)}
\chi_S\chi_A^*
\Bigg\}.
\end{align*}
Part~\textup{(i)} gives the signs of both coefficient families. For every
nonempty~$S\subseteq N(i)$,
\begin{equation*}
-\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)\geq0.
\end{equation*}
For the first family, when~$S=\vn$ the bound
$p_i^\star\leq\rho_i(\mb 0)$ gives
\begin{equation*}
\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(\vn)
-p_i^\star\widehat{\lambda_i}(\vn)
=
\lambda_i(\mb 0)\bb{\rho_i(\mb 0)-p_i^\star}
\geq0.
\end{equation*}
If~$S\neq\vn$ and~$\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)<0$, the definition of
$p_i^\star$ gives
\begin{equation*}
\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(S)
-p_i^\star\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)
\geq0.
\end{equation*}
If instead~$\widehat{\lambda_i}(S)=0$, the second coefficient inequality in
part~\textup{(i)} gives~$\widehat{\lambda_i\rho_i}(S)\geq0$. Hence every
coefficient multiplying a product~$\chi_S\chi_B^*$ in the preceding generator
formula is nonnegative.

It remains to expand those products in the centered basis. At each site,
\begin{equation*}
\eta(j)=p_j^\star+\bb{\eta(j)-p_j^\star},
\qquad
\eta(j)\bb{\eta(j)-p_j^\star}
=\bb{1-p_j^\star}\eta(j).
\end{equation*}
Applying the second identity on~$S\cap B$ and then the first on~$S$ gives
\begin{equation*}
\chi_S\chi_B^*
=
\bb{\prod_{j\in S\cap B}\bb{1-p_j^\star}}
\sum_{R\subseteq S}
\bb{\prod_{j\in S\setminus R}p_j^\star}
\chi_{R\cup(B\setminus S)}^*.
\end{equation*}
All coefficients are nonnegative because~$0\leq p_j^\star\leq1$. Thus every
term in~$\Lcal\chi_A^*$ other than the explicit negative multiple of
$\chi_A^*$ expands with nonnegative coefficients in the centered-monomial
basis. In particular, every off-diagonal coefficient of the generator is
nonnegative.

Fix now~$R\Subset\Lambda$. For the zero-boundary restriction to~$R$, setting
all exterior spins to~$0$ simply removes ordinary monomials~$\chi_S$ with
$S\nsubseteq R$; the surviving coefficients in the calculation above are
unchanged. Hence the finite-volume generator matrix~$M_R$ in the
centered-monomial basis has nonnegative off-diagonal entries. Since this matrix
is finite, we may choose~$a$ so that~$M_R+aI$ is entrywise nonnegative. Then
\begin{equation*}
e^{tM_R}
=
e^{-at}e^{t(M_R+aI)}
\end{equation*}
is entrywise nonnegative. Consequently, for every~$A\subseteq R$ there are
coefficients~$q_{A,B}^R(t)\geq0$ such that
\begin{equation*}
P_t^{R,0}\chi_A^*
=
\sum_{B\subseteq R}q_{A,B}^R(t)\chi_B^*.
\end{equation*}
If~$\mu\preceq_*\nu$, it follows that
\begin{equation*}
\bb{\nu P_t^{R,0}-\mu P_t^{R,0}}\bb{\chi_A^*}
=
\sum_{B\subseteq R}
q_{A,B}^R(t)\bb{\nu-\mu}\bb{\chi_B^*}
\geq0.
\end{equation*}
The finite-propagation approximation in
Appendix~\ref{app:finite-propagation} gives, for every finite~$A$ and fixed
$t$,
\begin{equation*}
\lim_{R\uparrow\Lambda}
(\mu P_t^{R,0})(\chi_A^*)
=
(\mu P_t)(\chi_A^*),
\end{equation*}
and the same for~$\nu$. Therefore
\begin{equation*}
\mu\preceq_*\nu
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mu P_t\preceq_*\nu P_t,
\end{equation*}
which proves the main assertion of part~\textup{(ii)}.
