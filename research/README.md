# Research workspace

This directory contains non-rendered working material for the autonomous research group.

It is scratch/research space, not part of the public wiki. Tentative claims, failed calculations, partial proofs, exploratory literature notes, computations, student work, Professor meeting notes, and handovers belong here rather than under `docs/`.

The group is organized around one persistent ChatGPT Professor and persistent graduate-student sessions.

For each new scientific direction:

1. the Professor creates a branch `research/<short-programme-slug>`;
2. create `research/active/<short-programme-slug>/`;
3. start from the templates under `research/templates/` when useful;
4. keep long mathematics in durable files rather than compressing it into chat handoffs;
5. keep `state.md` and `proof-spine.md` current enough for the Professor and students to re-ground themselves;
6. keep group-meeting notes under `meetings/`;
7. keep substantial student work under `students/` when separation is useful; and
8. use `handover.md` when a Professor or student session approaches a platform length limit.

A new graduate-student session is not created per task. The Professor reuses the student attached to the current direction. A new student is called only for a completely new scientific direction, or as the successor of the same student lineage when a platform session-length limit is reached.

Conversation links are optional lineage pointers only. They are not assumed to be readable by successor sessions. Exact transcript transfer plus repository handover is the fallback.

The templates are starting points, not mandatory response forms.
