# Group meeting

The Professor writes this after a substantial student handoff and after reading the decisive raw technical material.

The mathematical body is free-form. This is a research meeting, not a gate examination.

meeting_id:

evidence_pointer:

consecutive_no_narrowing_meetings_after_this_meeting:

## What the Professor actually read

List the exact student handoff, proof/counterexample/calculation files, failed-attempt material, proof-spine entries, and literature material that carried the assessment.

Do not base the meeting only on an optimistic student summary.

## What changed mathematically

State the actual change in the mathematical state.

`state_narrowed: yes` requires target-relevant uncertainty to have been reduced by something such as a proof, serious claimed lemma, counterexample, route elimination, sharper reduction or obstruction, improved controlling estimate, mathematically necessary hypothesis change, or material literature resolution.

A new speculative variant, more work with the same conclusion, or merely deciding what to try next does not by itself count as narrowing.

## Proof-spine changes

Record which load-bearing items changed status, were added, removed, or reformulated.

## Professor assessment

What is the current bottleneck?

What is the strongest reason to continue?

What is the strongest reason to doubt the direction?

Are the next proposed routes materially different from the routes that failed?

Has the expected value of another substantial work block increased, decreased, or stayed essentially unchanged?

If the group had not already invested effort here, would this still be the best available direction?

## Decision

Record the Professor's `continue`, `pivot`, or `close` decision and why.

The Professor may close on expected-value and opportunity-cost grounds. It does not need to prove that all conceivable approaches fail.

## Next assignment

Give the current graduate student a specified autonomous research objective and point to the relevant files.

Do not create a new student for a new lemma or variant.

## Outside consultation

Record any correctness, novelty, specialist, or stagnation consultation requested or answered.

After three consecutive `state_narrowed: no` meetings, Claude automatically requests a fresh outside Stagnation Consultant. The Professor must respond to that report at the next meeting but retains scientific authority.
