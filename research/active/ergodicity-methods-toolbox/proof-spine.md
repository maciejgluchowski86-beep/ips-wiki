# Coverage spine

This file replaces a theorem proof spine by a coverage map for the literature-compilation direction. It records method families that should eventually be represented in the toolbox and is revised after each literature batch.

## A. Coupling and influence

- basic/common graphical coupling and coupling inequalities;
- attractive/monotone coupling and extremal invariant measures;
- Dobrushin influence matrices and contraction;
- path coupling and local metric contraction;
- maximal/local coupling and block coupling;
- disagreement percolation / domination by branching or contact processes;
- coupling from the past / backward ancestor clans;
- information-percolation and history-cluster methods;
- censoring and monotone update comparison.

## B. Duality, graphical ancestry, and extinction

- self-/additive duality: extinction of a finite dual implies loss of memory;
- ancestor-cluster finiteness and clan-of-ancestors constructions;
- oriented-percolation comparison and subcritical dependency cones;
- complete-convergence/extremal-law arguments in attractive systems;
- regeneration/distinguished-particle or distinguished-vacancy constructions.

## C. Functional inequalities and semigroup decay

- Poincare/spectral-gap inequalities;
- logarithmic Sobolev and modified logarithmic Sobolev inequalities;
- entropy dissipation / approximate tensorization;
- Dirichlet-form comparison and comparison of generators;
- canonical paths / congestion bounds;
- block dynamics, martingale decomposition, bisection, multiscale variance decomposition;
- finite-volume criteria uniform in boundary conditions;
- perturbative coercivity / nonreversible hypocoercive variants when used for IPS-like chains.

## D. Spatial mixing to dynamical mixing

- Dobrushin uniqueness and Dobrushin-Shlosman conditions;
- strong spatial mixing / weak spatial mixing criteria;
- disagreement percolation as a Gibbs-to-dynamics bridge;
- spectral independence / influence bounds where used for Glauber dynamics;
- finite-volume boundary-condition comparison leading to spectral gap or mixing.

## E. Recurrence, Lyapunov, and regeneration

- Foster-Lyapunov drift plus minorization/Harris recurrence;
- small/petite set regeneration and Nummelin splitting;
- front/interface Lyapunov functions and extinction of disagreement;
- renewal structures and regeneration blocks;
- coupling/drift in Wasserstein or weighted metrics.

## F. KCSM- and model-specific relaxation mechanisms

- distinguished zero / oriented vacancy methods for East-type models;
- constrained Poincare inequalities and legal-path/canonical-path estimates;
- block renormalization and bisection for KCSM;
- bootstrap-percolation-assisted relaxation estimates;
- comparison with unconstrained refresh dynamics;
- model-specific dual, interface, or defect-particle representations that imply ergodicity or a gap.

## G. Finite-volume to infinite-volume transfer

- uniform finite-volume spectral gap/log-Sobolev estimates plus finite propagation;
- mixing in boxes with controlled boundary conditions;
- exhaustion and projective/compactness arguments for invariant laws;
- coupling/ancestor estimates uniform in volume;
- criteria turning local relaxation into uniqueness and convergence.

## H. Coverage discipline

The toolbox is an inventory, not a ranking. Overlap between families is expected; each live entry should identify related methods rather than duplicate their derivations. A method that is highly model-specific is retained if it has a rigorous source and a clear reusable mechanism.

The first wave covers representative items from A, B, C, D, E, and F. Later meetings should fill gaps rather than repeatedly expand already dense families.
