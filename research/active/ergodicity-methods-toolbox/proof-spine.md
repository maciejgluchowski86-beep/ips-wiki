## I. Coverage discipline

The toolbox is an inventory, not a ranking. Overlap between families is expected, but each live entry should expose a distinct reusable proof interface. Model-specific methods remain welcome when rigorous and clear.

Later assignments should preferentially fill uncovered families. Repeatedly adding variants inside already dense Poincare/LSI/basic-coupling territory is lower priority unless the mechanism is genuinely different.
