## Scientific target

For one-dimensional hard FA-1f with equilibrium vacancy density `q in (0,1)` and initial Bernoulli vacancy density `q0 in (0,1)`, prove local convergence of the Bernoulli quench to the equilibrium Bernoulli law.

The model has rate-one site clocks; at a legal ring, site `i` is refreshed to vacancy `0` with probability `q` and occupied `1` with probability `p=1-q`. A ring is legal iff at least one of `i-1,i+1` is vacant.

The toolbox synthesis retained one primary bridge, `FA-SCREEN`. For a fixed finite observation interval `A`, seek deterministic `s_t -> infinity`, events `E_t`, random times `tau_t <= t-s_t`, random finite intervals `I_t superset A`, and screen data `S_t` such that:

1. `P_{mu_{q0}}(E_t^c) -> 0`;
2. `|I_t|=o(s_t)` on `E_t`;
3. `E_t,S_t` are determined without revealing the protected future interior marks in `I_t x (tau_t,t]`;
4. conditional on the past and `S_t`, those protected interior marks are fresh and the evolution on `A` up to time `t` is insulated from the exterior except through boundary data to which finite-volume FA relaxation applies;
5. the conditional law in `I_t` at `tau_t` has `L^2(mu_q^{I_t})` cost at most `exp(C_q |I_t|)`.

Only after items 1--4 have a credible construction may the known positive finite-volume FA gap be used to close item 5/downstream relaxation.

## Part A. Leakage/measurability gate comes first

Work on the smallest concrete screen primitives before any tail or spectral-gap estimate.

Take a fixed protected core, e.g. `A={-1,0,1}`, and test one boundary at a time on a 5--7-site neighborhood. The left test is the reflection of the right test.

Start with the natural candidates suggested by East/front intuition:

1. **single distinguished vacancy:** a vacancy adjacent to the protected region whose marker may move outward;
2. **adjacent-vacancy dimer/corridor:** a boundary state containing two adjacent certified vacancies, with possible outward handoff when a new exterior vacancy is refreshed;
3. **finite boundary automaton:** the smallest finite state obtained from (1)--(2) by recording only boundary/exterior spins and graphical marks, never protected future interior marks.

For every candidate, specify precisely:

- the determining sigma-field;
- the protected marks that remain unrevealed;
- the screen state and its allowed transitions;
- what boundary datum the protected FA dynamics sees;
- the exact failure event.

Then exhaustively compare two continuations which agree on all declared screen data but differ in the protected interior and/or farther exterior. Reject the rule if either continuation can:

- change whether a boundary or protected interior ring is legal in a way not encoded by the screen datum;
- change a future screen-state transition;
- change whether screen success occurs;
- or make conditioning on success reveal any protected future interior clock/coin mark.

A failure caused merely by a particular choice of marker rule is not a theorem against all screening. Before stopping, test every genuinely different width-at-most-three local boundary state generated by the single-vacancy/dimer idea, modulo reflection and trivial relabeling. An exact finite enumerator is encouraged if useful.

### Faithfulness rule

Do not declare success by censoring actual rings, freezing a boundary spin by fiat, replacing the exterior facilitation by an independent signal, or conditioning on an event whose truth requires knowing protected interior legality. Any such construction fails the gate by definition.

## Part B. Persistence/scaling gate, only if Part A passes

If an explicit local screen state closes without leakage, study its lifetime/propagation using only the allowed boundary/exterior graphical randomness.

The question is whether two such screens can bracket `A` with probability tending to one while leaving a relaxation interval of length `s_t` and using total bracket width `o(s_t)`.

For a finite-state boundary screen, compute or bound its survival/handoff law exactly enough to decide whether `o(s_t)` many candidate boundary sites can plausibly produce a screen lasting order `s_t`. In particular, if every closed local screen has an exponential lifetime tail with rate bounded below at some `q>0`, then a sublinear-width search cannot make screen success tend to one; prove that implication rather than proceeding to the spectral gap.

If the rule branches into several certified boundary vacancies, identify the exact lower process. Do not silently replace it by an exogenous contact process unless there is a pathwise domination in the required direction.
