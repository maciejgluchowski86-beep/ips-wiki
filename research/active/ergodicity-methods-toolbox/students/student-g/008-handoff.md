## Five rulings

1. **East distinguished-zero screening — PASS.** The literal East lemma fails because orientation is load-bearing, but the Professor's strengthened bridge asks for a genuinely new two-sided approximate causal screen with fresh interior randomness and sublinear width. No existing chronology/dual obstruction rules that out, and once such a screen exists the known positive FA gap really does finish the quench.

2. **Refined non-diagonal discrepancy coupling — DEMOTE.** The Gobron--Saada theorem itself relies on conservative exclusion moves plus monotonicity and does not transfer. More importantly, an unrestricted measure-preserving injection between the two endpoint-history events is essentially equivalent to the desired cross-product inequality. Until a finite local/predictable switch state closes under every adjacent FA update, this is a search primitive rather than a target-level architecture.

3. **Information percolation/backward histories — PASS.** Standard mark-only Lubetzky--Sly update supports cannot work because hard FA has no state-independent oblivious marks. A quench-specific adaptive reveal process is nevertheless genuinely different: actual vacancy discoveries can short-circuit branches and merge dependencies, and no exact result identifies this minimal causal information with the conservative transformed dual. Vanishing adaptive-history influence would directly compare `mu_{q0}` with stationary `mu_q` under the same graphical marks.

4. **Front regeneration/renewal — DEMOTE.** The source relies on a canonical physical front separating explored from fresh material; the translation-invariant Bernoulli quench has no such interface. Once the proposed two-front bridge is strengthened enough to prevent re-entry and provide a fresh bracket, it is the same causal-screen theorem as Candidate 1 rather than an independent B-level architecture. Regeneration remains a useful implementation template and is more natural for the finite-seed problem.

5. **State-dependent dynamical disagreement percolation — KILL.** The proposed block process lacks an iteratable subcritical domination. At positive times the quench environment is endogenous and nonstationary, and conditioning on disagreement survival biases the vacancy field. Unconditional product-background transmission estimates therefore cannot be iterated. Uniform conditional bounds revert to worst-case disagreement percolation and conflict with the all-ones trap; law-dependent bounds require a separate screening/regeneration or adaptive-history theorem first.
