# Weighted Wasserstein contraction for infinite particle systems

## Criterion

Let \(V\) be countable and let configurations \(\eta=(\eta(x))_{x\in V}\) have nonnegative continuous spins. Choose positive weights \(v(x)\) and the tempered state space
\[
\mathcal X=\left\{\eta:\|\eta\|_v:=\sum_{x\in V}v(x)\eta(x)<\infty\right\}.
\]
Equip probability laws with finite first moment with the Wasserstein distance induced by \(\|\cdot\|_v\).

Bezborodov--Di Persio--Friesen--Kuchling consider a broad interacting jump-diffusion/branching system on this space. Under their structural assumptions (A1)--(A6), Theorem 1.1 gives a pathwise comparison principle: ordered initial configurations, driven by the common noises of the stochastic equation, remain ordered. Write \(\widetilde B(x,\eta)\) for the effective drift, including the mean contribution of jump mechanisms. Theorem 1.2 assumes in addition that for some \(A>0\), for every ordered pair \(\xi\le\eta\),
\[
\sum_{x\in V}v(x)\bigl(\widetilde B(x,\eta)-\widetilde B(x,\xi)\bigr)
\le -A\|\eta-\xi\|_v.
\]
Then for all probability laws \(\rho,\widetilde\rho\) with finite first moment,
\[
W_1(P_t^*\rho,P_t^*\widetilde\rho)
\le e^{-At}W_1(\rho,\widetilde\rho).
\]
In particular there is a unique invariant law \(\pi\) in this class and \(P_t^*\rho\) converges to \(\pi\) exponentially in \(W_1\).

## Mechanism

The distance is adapted to an infinite system rather than being an unweighted finite-volume Hamming distance. The weights make configurations with infinitely many nonzero coordinates admissible while controlling their influence at spatial infinity.

For ordered deterministic starts, the comparison coupling gives \(\xi_t\le\eta_t\). Positivity removes the absolute value coordinate by coordinate, so the expected weighted distance is exactly the weighted difference of first moments. The effective-drift inequality therefore implies
\[
\mathbb E\|\eta_t-\xi_t\|_v
\le \|\eta_0-\xi_0\|_v-A\int_0^t
\mathbb E\|\eta_s-\xi_s\|_v\,ds,
\]
and Gronwall yields exponential contraction. Section 7 extends this estimate from ordered deterministic pairs to arbitrary initial laws by inserting ordered envelopes and then optimizing over initial couplings.

Thus the proof interface is a **contractive weighted transportation metric generated by a common stochastic forcing**. It does not require contraction of every individual spin update, a finite configuration space, or a shortest-path extension from neighboring configurations.

## Representative IPS use

The paper's class includes infinite-type continuous-state branching processes with immigration, interacting branching systems and population models on countable graphs. Example 2.3 treats an infinite-type continuous-state branching process with immigration. When the weighted interaction/migration coefficients are dominated by the local dissipative term by a positive margin, the criterion of Theorem 1.2 applies and the infinite-dimensional process converges to its unique invariant distribution.

This is an IPS-like application in which ordinary finite Hamming path coupling is not even the natural state-space geometry: each site carries a nonnegative mass and infinitely many coordinates may be active.

## Limitations

The theorem is a subcritical strong-dissipativity criterion. The effective drift must contract the weighted total discrepancy uniformly by a positive rate; the authors note that this simple condition is sufficient rather than optimal. It therefore excludes regimes with multiple invariant laws and can fail near criticality even when uniqueness remains true. The comparison principle also uses monotonicity of the coefficients and the positive cone; systems without an order compatible with common-noise coupling need different Wasserstein arguments. Finally, convergence is in \(W_1\) on the finite-first-moment class, not automatically total variation, spectral-gap, or entropy convergence.
