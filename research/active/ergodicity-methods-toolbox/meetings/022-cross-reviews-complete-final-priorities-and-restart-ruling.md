### FA-1f / East

The final ranking has two surviving architectures.

**First: two-sided causal screening.** The literal East distinguished-vacancy theorem does not transfer because FA facilitation is two-sided. The surviving bridge instead asks for an explicit high-probability causal screen whose success can be determined without revealing the protected future marks, whose protected interval has width `o(s_t)`, and which leaves enough fresh time `s_t` for the already-known positive finite-volume FA gap to erase the conditional initial density cost.

This bridge clears the assessment restart bar because:

- its load-bearing sigma-field/freshness statement is stronger and more falsifiable than the desired convergence conclusion;
- no chronology/dual obstruction rules it out;
- once it is proved, the downstream theorem chain is short and uses known FA coercivity rather than new equilibrium analysis.

**Second: adaptive information histories.** This remains the independent reserve architecture. It must use a quench-specific state-adaptive reveal process, not mark-only Ising update supports and not the conservative transformed dual. The target is a second-moment/pair-intersection bound on residual time-zero information sufficient for local total-variation convergence.

The chronology-switch candidate is demoted to a finite local feasibility primitive. Front regeneration is demoted to a possible implementation of the causal screen rather than a separate Bernoulli-quench architecture. State-dependent disagreement percolation is killed as an independent route because an unconditional product-background transmission estimate is not iterable under path conditioning; any repair first needs screening or adaptive-history control.

**Ruling:** recommend reopening the FA Bernoulli-quench programme for one bounded block on the two-sided causal-screen theorem, beginning with the finite leakage/measurability gate. Do not run a separate full adaptive-history programme simultaneously unless the principal chooses that parallelism after the first gate.

### Positive rates

The three PASS candidates reduce to two families.

**First: nonbasic one-dimensional coupling.** The exact first bridge is a genuinely new finite-range Markovian coupling, allowed to pair different microscopic moves, with uniform negative Hamming drift on every finite-disagreement pair. This is stronger than necessary but cheaply falsifiable. If the local coupled-rate constraints are feasible, add Gray-style no-crossing, permanent-coalescence, and protected-region constraints on the same variables. Gray is the structural target, not a separate first block.

**Second: information percolation.** Strict positivity gives genuine state-independent reset maps, so a minimal backward-history representation exists in a way unavailable for hard FA. The target is a pair-intersection/red-history estimate, not total ancestor extinction or a first-moment branching bound.

The block/Bellman route is demoted because its proposed bridge is equivalent, through the exact scale-extension identity, to the desired diameter contraction unless an independent cross-block theorem is supplied. The disagreement-front regeneration route is demoted because Meeting 019 already exposed hidden right-ancestry not retained by the local front state and stopped the common-uniform global-coalescence interface.

**Ruling:** do not reopen the positive-rates proof programme yet. Authorize, if the principal wishes, only the two bounded feasibility experiments recorded in the final synthesis: the exact rational non-diagonal coupled-rate/Gray LP at `P_h`, and the optimized finite-depth random-map pair-support/intersection calculation. A positive structural signal from either is required before a new infinite-volume block.
