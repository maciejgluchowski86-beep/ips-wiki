## Ruling

**FA-INFO-002 ends `STOP-NO-ITERABLE-STATE`.**

State-adaptive causal evaluation genuinely prunes the mark-only support, and the exact terminal channel genuinely cancels likelihood information. But the two features do not coexist in a bounded compositional state:

- retaining the adaptive transcript or enough shared graphical history to compose produces a noncontractive pair/likelihood quantity;
- averaging enough information to recover contraction leaves a terminal statistic whose next constrained update depends on new higher-order joint information;
- exact adjacent staircase composition creates nonzero centered correlation order at every depth.

The only exact repair visible is an enlarging transcript/correlation hierarchy, which the assignment explicitly forbids after the finite gate fails.

This is not an impossibility theorem for every conceivable adaptive-information proof. It is a rigorous stop of the concrete A/B architecture that survived the hostile toolbox review.

## 1. Exact adaptive likelihood survives value-dependent querying

For a predictable adaptive transcript

\[
Q=(i_1,b_1,\ldots,i_K,b_K),
\]

with repeated bottom coordinates cached,

\[
\boxed{
L(Q)=
\prod_{j=1}^{K}
\left(\frac{q_0}{q}\right)^{1-b_j}
\left(\frac{1-q_0}{1-q}\right)^{b_j}.}
\]

The adaptive query-set dependence does not invalidate the product likelihood because each next index is selected from already revealed information. It **does** invalidate conditioning only on the final random query set: set membership is value-biased.

Thus the usual mark-only intersection-count formula cannot simply be quoted.

For one terminal output `Y`, an exact two-copy replacement is

\[
1+\chi^2(\Law_{q_0}(Y),\Law_q(Y))
=E\left[
L(Q)L(Q')
\frac{\mathbf1_{\{Y=Y'\}}}{P_q(Y)}
\right]
\]

for two independent reference experiments.

This is accepted as a useful exact likelihood lemma independently of the route decision.

\[
E[V_0'\mid V_{-1},V_0,V_1]

\[
\mathcal X_1=(1-q_0)^4\mathcal C_0.

\[

=\frac{130321}{160000}<1
\quad(q_0=1/20),

The route therefore encounters a genuine **cancellation-before-transcript** phenomenon: the local output forgets more than any positive certificate/transcript bound sees.

## 8. Exact growing-correlation theorem

For a right-to-left staircase of `m` equilibrium-coin rings at sites `m-1,...,0`, define conditional mean vacancies by

\[
M_m=V_m,
\]

\[
M_k
=q+(V_k-q)(1-V_{k-1})(1-M_{k+1}).
\]

Then the coefficient of

\[
\prod_{j=-1}^{m}(V_j-q)
\]

in `M_0-q` is exactly

\[
\boxed{(-q)^{m-1}.}
\]

It is nonzero at every depth.

At S2 this gives a four-body coefficient `-q`. There are two positive four-site laws with identical every proper marginal but different S2 output, obtained by the small perturbations

\[
\frac{d\nu_\pm}{d\mu_q^4}
=1\pm\varepsilon
\frac{\prod_{j=-1}^{2}(V_j-q)}{(q(1-q))^2}.
\]

Therefore no state retaining only a fixed finite collection of lower-order local marginals can exactly compose the sharp output statistic. The staircase theorem shows the exact hierarchy continues to grow.
