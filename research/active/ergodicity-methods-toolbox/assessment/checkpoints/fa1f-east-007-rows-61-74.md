| 61 | Asymptotic strong Feller support uniqueness | X | Bernoulli equilibrium `mu_q` has full topological support, while hard FA also has the distinct invariant trap `delta_1`; hence a support-separation criterion strong enough to imply uniqueness cannot hold on the full FA state space. | 62 | Hörmander--Malliavin propagation to ASF | N | Bracket-generated Malliavin covariance, differentiable initial-condition directions and parabolic high-mode dissipation have no analogue in the discrete hard-constrained FA process. Pointer: `hormander-malliavin-asf-semilinear-spde.md`. |

| 64 | Successful coupling of fixed-size finite duals | X | The source requires a dual preserving particle number and then couples within each fixed sector. The fixed-sector hypothesis therefore does not match the live FA dual. Pointer: `successful-coupling-finite-dual-particles.md`; finite-seed obstruction record. |
| 65 | Voter coalescing-walk duality | N | FA backward histories are not single-parent coalescing random walks; legal refreshes depend on a two-sided vacancy constraint and the known finite-set dual branches/changes support. Pointer: `voter-coalescing-random-walk-duality.md`. |
| 66 | Parity branching-annihilating duality | N | FA is not a cancellative voter-type system with parity duality. No parity observable connects to the quench sign targets. Pointer: `parity-duality-branching-annihilating.md`. |
| 67 | Supercritical block construction / complete convergence | C | A coarse process of vacancy-rich blocks could prove robust propagation of activity, but activity/survival alone is not the missing theorem: FA already has positive-density vacancies under the quench and positive equilibrium gap. To reach convergence, a block event would need an additional restart/screening statement making its core forget the exterior; that is row 51/73 rather than supplied by the block-percolation theorem. Pointer: `supercritical-block-construction-complete-convergence.md`. |
| 68 | Two-level block-and-restart complete convergence | C | Seed-to-seed restart plus a forward/backward meeting argument is a useful complete-convergence template, but its source depends on monotone contact-type survival and a compatible backward dual. Pointer: `two-level-contact-block-restart-complete-convergence.md`. |
| 69 | Toom error-graph expansion | N | The theorem needs a low-noise perturbation of an eroding monotone deterministic PCA and charges reconstructed historical errors by a small parameter. FA has no such deterministic eroder/noise perturbation regime, so the error-entropy balance has no target quantity to exploit. Pointer: `toom-error-graph-expansion-pca.md`. |
| 70 | Coupling from the past | X | CFTP requires eventual coalescence of the common finite-state random map. Hard FA has a persistent all-ones closed class and is non-attractive, so global coalescence to the Bernoulli equilibrium state cannot occur. Pointer: `coupling-from-the-past.md`. |
| 71 | Clan-of-ancestors perfect simulation | X | A stationary perfect-simulation construction based on almost-sure finite backward dependency clans would select a unique local state independently of the remote past. Hard FA has a blocked all-ones stationary history, so no such full-state-space criterion can hold. A *law-dependent sparse history* criterion is still possible and is exactly why row 72 is different. Pointer: `clan-of-ancestors-perfect-simulation.md`. |
| 72 | Information percolation / backward histories | B | **High-priority law-dependent memory architecture.** Unlike CFTP or finite-clan extinction, information percolation permits backward histories to reach time zero; only the histories that still carry initial information must be sparse enough. This is compatible with the existence of the all-ones trap because the desired estimate is under the Bernoulli quench/stationary laws, not worst-case over starts. A FA adaptation would reveal the minimal legal-update support of a fixed observation block backward in time, condition on histories whose spin is determined by refresh randomness, and show that the remaining red/time-zero-dependent clusters have vanishing contribution to local observables. This bypasses positive coefficient-transfer contraction and directly attacks initial-memory survival. Pointer: `information-percolation-backward-histories.md`; chronology target ledger. Explicit bridge lemma in final shortlist. |
| 73 | East distinguished-zero screening | A | **Closest solved architecture and strongest candidate.** East proves the exact missing two-step pattern: a moving vacancy supplies conditional equilibrium behind it, then the positive gap relaxes the remaining local error. FA already has the second step (positive gap for every `q>0`); what is missing is a two-sided replacement for the exact East screening lemma. A single suitable approximate/local regeneration-screen lemma around a fixed observation interval would close the Bernoulli quench. It must retain endogenous two-sided facilitation rather than exogenize the boundary signals. Pointer: `east-distinguished-zero-screening.md`; `kcsm-constraint-domination-reference-process.md`; chronology equations (12d)--(12f). Explicit bridge and falsification test in final shortlist. It is also the dominant template for the finite-seed problem. |
| 74 | Potential-theoretic capacity / metastability | N | Capacity estimates rare transitions between finite-volume valleys. The FA quench is not a metastable escape problem and the all-ones trap is genuinely absorbing rather than a rare valley from which capacity induces escape. Pointer: `potential-theoretic-capacity-metastability.md`. |
