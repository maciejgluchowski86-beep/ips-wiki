1. the distinguished-zero path is determined from the unscreened side and does not reveal protected marks;
2. between marker moves, the protected region evolves with a fixed zero boundary;
3. the released site at a marker move is incorporated with the correct equilibrium law;
4. the equilibrium family is consistent as the protected interval grows.

The positive-rates programme already has an exact candidate replacement for ingredient 2. If `pi_N` is the unique invariant law of the `N`-site zero-boundary chain, one-sidedness gives exact right-suffix projectivity

`R_{N,M} pi_N = pi_M`.

`bar pi_{N+1}=pi_N`.
