## New input from the principal exploration

The principal's separate exploration recovers a different all-depth object from the centered monomial dual. It is not the cellwise positivity route closed at Meeting 002.

For a finite interval `R=[ell,r]`, on the event that an active successful interaction exits `R`, there is a canonical predecessor trail from a unique root `x in A` to the final exit. If `n=r-x+1` and the selected times are

$$
0<t_1<\cdots<t_n=\tau_R(t),
$$

then the trail, left region, and right region use disjoint Poisson families after conditioning on the decorated trail. In the residual centered dual, all selected trail interactions are births and the complete vertical factor is

$$
\boxed{e^{-\omega\tau_R(t)},\qquad \omega=1-c+a>0.}
$$

The resulting nonempty-trail contribution is

$$
E_A[W_t^\eta;\tau_R(t)>0]
=
\sum_{x\in A}B(b-a)^{n_x-1}
\int_{\Delta_{n_x}(t)}
 e^{-\omega t_{n_x}}
 L_\gamma(\eta)
 [\rho U_{\gamma,0}(\eta)+q_*U_{\gamma,1}(\eta)]\,dt,
$$

with

$$
B=b+c-a,
\qquad
\rho=\frac cB,
\qquad
q_* = \frac{b-a}{B}.
$$

The Professor independently checked the centered-dual algebra carrying this formula:

$$

\qquad

\delta_* = \frac{b(1-c)-a}{b-a},

and the trail exponent identity. In the signed-death side `delta_*<0`, using rate `|delta_*|` gives the same cancellation and leaves `e^{-\omega\tau}`. The exact Poisson-Mecke factorization is accepted as a working lemma for the research spine; if it becomes part of a closing proof it will need the usual independent proof audit.

## Left finite-volume relaxation and the actual remaining criterion

Write the total trail duration as `tau=|u|` and the lag after the final exit as `s=t-tau`. The left contribution has the form

$$
L_{\gamma,t}=P_s^{<r+1,0}F_{x,u},
$$

where `F_{x,u}` is generated by alternating zero-boundary semigroups and multiplication by centered characters at successive trail sites.

For fixed `R`, the zero-boundary chain on the finite relevant interval is irreducible at every strict residual parameter point, hence relaxes uniformly to its invariant law `pi^0_{m,r}`. The mixing time may depend arbitrarily badly on `R`: the intended order is `t to infinity` first, then `R to infinity`.

The recent-exit region `s<T_R` has `tau>t-T_R`. Because `omega>0`, its fixed-depth simplex mass is exponentially small in `t`. Thus the nonempty-trail term is reduced to

$$
\boxed{
B(b-a)^{n-1}
\int_{(0,\infty)^n}
 e^{-\omega|u|}
 |\pi^0_{m,r}(F_{x,u})|\,du
\longrightarrow0
\qquad(n=r-x+1\to\infty).
}
\tag{L}
$$

This is a sharper all-depth target than the present disagreement-stack formulation: it asks for decay of one specific family of centered invariant expectations rather than extinction of all possible coupled stacks.

The complementary no-exit term still has to be checked explicitly when the full proof is reconstructed. The principal exploration treats it as part of the existing finite-volume barrier argument; the next student assignment must not silently omit it.

## Exact East structural cancellation

At the exact East heat-bath model with zero facilitating boundary, the relevant finite-volume invariant law is Bernoulli product. The final selected trail interaction is a birth, so

$$
F_{x,u}=h_{p_*}(\eta_r)G_{x,u}(\eta_{<r}),
$$

and therefore

$$
\boxed{\pi^0_{m,r}(F_{x,u})=0.}
$$

The equilibrium contribution at East is exactly zero, not merely small. This is structurally stronger evidence for the centered-transfer route than a crude density comparison.

The exact East point has `omega=0`, so the recent-exit exponential argument is unavailable there. That is consistent: East is the singular structural limit, not a strict positive-rate residual point.

## Sufficient all-depth transfer theorem

Define on the generated signed-measure class

$$
(\mathcal C_{y,u}\nu)(f)
=
\nu(h_{p_*}(\eta_y)P_u^{<y,0}f).
$$

A sufficient theorem is a norm and `theta<1` such that

$$
\boxed{
(b-a)\int_0^\infty e^{-\omega u}
\|\mathcal C_{y,u}\nu\|_{*,y-1}\,du
\le
\theta\|\nu\|_{*,y}
}
\tag{T}
$$

uniformly in depth and interval on the signed measures actually generated by the trail. Iteration gives exponential decay in (L).

Total variation on all signed measures cannot work because the centered multiplier has size `1/q_*`; the norm must preserve centering cancellation.

Along

$$
a=\varepsilon^2,
\qquad b=\varepsilon,
\qquad c=1-\varepsilon^2,
$$

the one-site constant-mode factor is exactly

$$
\frac{1-\varepsilon}{2(1+\varepsilon)}<\frac12.
$$

So the scale needed by (T) is compatible with the East limit at one level.
