## Why this consultation exists

The group has now exhausted the current centered predecessor-trail/profile implementation by a pre-registered stopping rule.

Its two load-bearing interfaces both terminate at uncontrolled zero-frequency spatial memory:

1. the positive common-uniform disagreement interface reduces to all-depth disagreement occupation / source-return capacity;
2. the recombined signed-profile interface reduces, even after fixed-suffix localization and finite propagation, to one- or two-step tail-shift defects of the projective zero-boundary invariant law.

The latest exact reduction is

$$
\Gamma_M
\le
c^2 Z\,\Delta_{\lceil M/2\rceil}^{(2)}
+Ce^{-\gamma M},
$$

where

$$
\Delta_M^{(2)}
=\|\theta^2\mu-\mu\|_{\mathcal F_M}.
$$

The group is **not** authorizing another request to prove this by generic mixing, observability, spectral gaps, common-uniform extinction, a third insertion, a matrix-product norm, or a larger finite-state truncation.

The question is whether there is now a genuinely different proof architecture with enough concrete structure to justify another internal block.

## Consultation objective

Identify the strongest credible route that is mathematically distinct from the exhausted implementation, and if possible prove the first nontrivial theorem on that route.

You should explicitly test at least the following possibilities, but you need not spend equal effort on them:

### A. Direct spatial stationary-law theorem

The finite zero-boundary invariant laws `pi_N` are suffix-projective. Is there a direct spatial recursion, transfer operator, conditional kernel, entropy/information contraction, renewal representation, or one-sided stationarity identity which proves asymptotic shift invariance of the projective half-line law **without** assuming dynamical mixing or global common-uniform extinction?

A proposal to prove `Delta_M->0` or `Delta_M^(2)->0` counts as a new route only if you exhibit such an independent mechanism. Merely restating tail-shift agreement, invoking an unknown spectral gap, or proposing generic matrix-product approximation does not count.

### B. Different disagreement / coupling representation

Is there a coupling, information-percolation, backward exploration, disagreement percolation, censoring, or regeneration construction which is genuinely different from the common-uniform global-coalescence interface and avoids the all-depth source-episode obstruction?

It must preserve the exact IPS law. A successful route need not be monotone, synchronous, or Hamming-based.

### C. Different dual / transform / density-profile representation

Can a different signed duality, density-profile dynamics, change of basis, nonlinear transform, or backward random map produce a norm or cancellation theorem that does not reduce to the closed scalar Foster classes, fixed finite generator modes, or the present predecessor-trail `J` quantity?

If so, define the object exactly and derive at least one load-bearing estimate rather than proposing an ansatz family.

### D. Route-killing conclusion

If the natural alternatives above all reduce to one of the already isolated zero-frequency problems or require a theorem essentially equivalent to the positive-rates conjecture, say so explicitly. The correct recommendation may be that no further internal block is presently justified.
