# Student G 011b: buffered distinguished-zero screening is exactly the tail-shift defect

## Result

The natural buffered repair of the failed exact East induction does not create a new screening quantity. Its sharp measure-level error is exactly the already-isolated tail-shift defect `Delta_M`, up to the one-site indexing convention for whether the newly released marker site is counted as part of the buffer.

Suppose a marker move enlarges an old screened block of `N` sites to `N+1` sites. The old `N` coordinates are not inspected or modified by the move. Allow the last `m` sites of that old block to be declared contaminated, and retain only the protected prefix

$$
U_{N,m}=\{1,\ldots,N-m\}.
$$

Define the sharp buffered compatibility error

$$
S_m
:=
\sup_{N\ge m+1}
\sup_{\substack{|f|\le1\\
\operatorname{supp}(f)\subseteq U_{N,m}}}
\left|
\bar\pi_{N+1}(f)-\pi_N(f)
\right|.
$$

Then

$$
\boxed{S_m=\Delta_{m+1}.}
$$

If the convention calls the newly released coordinate itself the first site of a width-`M` boundary layer, then the same identity is written `S=Delta_M`. There is no mathematical distinction in the asymptotic question.

Consequently a buffered screen with `m->infinity` and error tending to zero is equivalent to

$$
\Delta_M\longrightarrow0,
$$

which Meeting 014 already identifies with shift agreement of the projective half-line zero-boundary invariant law on its remote-left tail sigma-field.

## Proof of the exact identity

Meeting 014 defines

$$
\Delta_M
=
\sup_{K\ge M+1}
\sup_{\substack{|f|\le1\\
\operatorname{supp}(f)\subseteq\{1,\ldots,K-M\}}}
\left|
\bar\pi_K(f)-\pi_{K-1}(f)
\right|.
$$

Set `K=N+1` and `M=m+1`. Then

$$
K-M=(N+1)-(m+1)=N-m,
$$

and the displayed definition becomes exactly `S_m`.

No estimate is used.

## Conditioning on a distinguished-marker path does not improve this gate

Let `G` be a sigma-field generated by marker/right-side graphical information, chosen so that the protected old prefix is not inspected and its protected-side graphical marks remain fresh. This is the analogue of the East measurability requirement.

If before the move the old block has conditional law `pi_N` given `G`, and the move leaves the protected prefix untouched, then every bounded `U_{N,m}`-measurable test function `f` satisfies

$$
E[f\mid G]=\pi_N(f).
$$

Any exact target `pi_{N+1}` for the enlarged screened block would instead require the same protected observable to have expectation `bar pi_{N+1}(f)`. Therefore the conditional compatibility error is still bounded below, and in the sharp uniform formulation equals, the same prefix discrepancy above. Right-side conditioning can choose when the comparison is made, but it cannot change the marginal of coordinates it neither inspects nor modifies.

Thus the geometry does not manufacture an improved **instantaneous invariant-family compatibility** estimate.

## Local-observable weakening does not close an East induction by itself

For one fixed local observable `f`, one can of course ask only for

$$
|\bar\pi_{N+1}(f)-\pi_N(f)|
$$

rather than the full protected-prefix variation. That is weaker than `Delta_{m+1}`. But an East-style screening induction must propagate the conditional screened law through a nonzero future time. The semigroup of a local observable depends, by finite propagation, on an increasing causal cone. As the available relaxation time grows, the class of protected functions that must be controlled grows with that cone.

There are then only two ways already present in the stopped programme to make this local weakening useful:

1. assume/control the invariant prefix discrepancy uniformly on the growing cone, which is exactly the `Delta_M`/zero-frequency boundary-response problem; or
2. prove a new dynamical contraction/cancellation theorem that suppresses the boundary discrepancy before it reaches the observation set.

Meeting 014 gives the existing first option explicitly: for a one-segment evolved test function it bounds the boundary response by `Delta_{M-d}` plus a finite-propagation error. The connected-renewal programme's only surviving cancellation-based version of the second option is Meeting 030's signed boundary-transmission operator `(V)`. The abandoned common-uniform all-depth theorem is another already-stopped dynamical route.

No new graphical contraction is supplied merely by naming the marker. Hence a statement of the form "choose a wide enough contaminated layer so that the screened prefix is approximately in the next `pi` law" is not new input: its exact sharp hypothesis is `Delta_M->0`.
