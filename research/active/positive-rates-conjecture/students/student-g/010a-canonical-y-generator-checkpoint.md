# Student G 010a checkpoint: canonical `Y` generator and a one-step norm obstruction

**Status:** intermediate durable checkpoint for Assignment 010. This does **not** decide `(J-SPEC)` or the connected-tail target `(T)`.

The point of this checkpoint is to put the connected orbit in the basis naturally selected by the insertion itself and to rule out the first tempting depth-uniform coefficient norm before spending the block on a stronger multi-step/sign-sensitive estimate.

## 1. Canonical insertion variable

Write

\[
k:=1-c,
\qquad
B=1+b-a-k,
\qquad
g=b-a,
\qquad
Y_i:=B\eta_i-c.
\]

Thus `Y_i` takes the values `-c` and `g`, and the connected insertion is exactly multiplication by `Y_i`; there is no affine remainder in this basis.

Put

\[
d:=bk-a=b(1-c)-a.
\]

For an interior site `i`, direct evaluation on the four states of `(eta_i,eta_{i+1})` gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
L_iY_i=d-(b+k)Y_i+Y_iY_{i+1}.
}
\tag{1}
\]

At the zero right boundary, where `Y_{N+1}=-c`, the same formula gives

\[
\boxed{
L_NY_N=d-(1+b)Y_N.
}
\tag{2}
\]

No approximation or East limit is used here.

The two-point relation is

\[
Y_i^2=(g-c)Y_i+cg.
\tag{3}
\]

Consequently, for `Y_A:=\prod_{j\in A}Y_j`, the local action at `i in A` is

\[
L_iY_A=
\begin{cases}
 dY_{A\setminus\{i\}}-(b+k)Y_A+Y_{A\cup\{i+1\}},
 &i<N,\ i+1\notin A,\\[1mm]
 dY_{A\setminus\{i\}}-(1+a)Y_A+cg\,Y_{A\setminus\{i+1\}},
 &i<N,\ i+1\in A,\\[1mm]
 dY_{A\setminus\{N\}}-(1+b)Y_A,
 &i=N.
\end{cases}
\tag{4}
\]

This is the exact coefficient-space dynamics relevant to the connected resolvents. Apart from the single coefficient `d`, every off-diagonal coefficient in `(4)` is nonnegative. In particular, at the primary point

\[
P_*=(a,b,c)=\left(\frac1{1000},\frac1{10},\frac{9999}{10000}\right)
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{
d=-\frac{99}{100000}.}
\tag{5}
\]

Thus the soft perturbation of the positive East-type coefficient process is an explicit **negative annihilation term**, not an unspecified growing-mode error.

Equivalently, if `D Y_A=\sum_{i\in A}Y_{A\setminus\{i\}}`, then

\[
L=L^{+}+dD,
\tag{6}
\]

where `L^+` is the operator obtained from `(4)` by setting `d=0`; `L^+` is Metzler in the monomial basis.

## 2. Why the obvious degree-weighted `ell^1` norm cannot be the all-depth theorem

For `theta>0`, consider the coefficient norm

\[
\left\|\sum_A q_A Y_A\right\|_\theta
:=\sum_A \theta^{|A|}|q_A|.
\tag{7}
\]

A natural hope is that the actual coefficient semigroup generated by `(4)` is nonexpansive in one such norm, after which one would estimate the signed filter resolvent and the insertion. This cannot hold uniformly in depth at `P_*`.

Let

\[
e:=|d|=\frac{99}{100000}.
\]

### Isolated-particle necessary condition

Take, in a volume whose right boundary lies farther right,

\[
A_n=\{1,3,5,\ldots,2n-1\}.
\]

All occupied sites have an empty right neighbour. The off-diagonal terms generated from distinct sites are distinct, so the right derivative at time zero of the norm of `e^{tL}Y_{A_n}` is exactly

\[
n\theta^{n}
\left[
\theta+\frac e\theta-(b+k)
\right].
\]

Uniform nonexpansiveness therefore requires

\[
\theta+\frac e\theta\le b+k=\frac{1001}{10000}.
\tag{8}
\]

Hence

\[
\theta\le
\frac{1001+\sqrt{606001}}{20000}
<0.089.
\tag{9}
\]

### Long-block necessary condition

Now take `A_n={1,2,...,n}` in a volume with one further site to the right. For each removal of an interior site `j>=2`, the positive coalescence contribution `cg` from `j-1` and the negative annihilation contribution `d=-e` from `j` land on the same monomial and combine to `cg-e>0`. The first removal has coefficient `-e`; the only birth is from the right edge. Therefore, after division by `theta^n`, the time-zero norm derivative is

\[
-(n-1)(1+a)-(b+k)
+\theta+\frac e\theta
+(n-1)\frac{cg-e}{\theta}.
\]

Letting `n->infinity`, uniform nonexpansiveness requires

\[
\theta\ge\frac{cg-e}{1+a}.
\tag{10}
\]

At `P_*`,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{cg-e}{1+a}
=\frac{89091}{910000}
>0.0979.
}
\tag{11}
\]

The requirements `(9)` and `(11)` are incompatible. Hence:

\[
\boxed{
\text{No norm of the form `(7)` makes the canonical `Y` coefficient semigroup
nonexpansive uniformly in depth at `P_*`.}
}
\tag{12}
\]

This obstruction uses the actual signed generator, including the cancellation `cg+d=cg-e`; it is not an artifact of replacing all off-diagonal entries by their absolute values.

## 3. Consequence for Assignment 010

The canonical `Y` basis is still useful because every connected insertion simply appends the newest `Y` factor, while `(1)`--`(6)` isolate the only sign-indefinite local transition. But `(12)` rules out the simplest one-step positive coefficient Lyapunov estimate. Any successful tail theorem must retain additional structure, for example a bounded-step/sign-sensitive norm which distinguishes component geometry, or an exact resolvent identity exploiting cancellation before absolute values.

This is sharper than F009's growing-mode obstruction: even allowing the full growing monomial space, the most natural depth-independent degree weight cannot produce the required one-step contraction. It does **not** rule out the multi-step connected mechanism authorized in Assignment 010.
