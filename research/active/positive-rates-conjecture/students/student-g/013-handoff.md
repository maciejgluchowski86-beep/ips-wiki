## Decisive theorem

A faithful Gray/Harris grand coupling uses common local graphical events. Conditional on one event type, the update at a site is a deterministic Boolean rule

\[
F:\{0,1\}^2\to\{0,1\}
\]

acting consistently on both source copies and their half-line hybrids.

Take only Gray's two extremal source configurations and the two orientations of a single half-line splice. Exact hybrid identity plus scalar closure at the first event at the splice boundary forces

\[
\boxed{F(0,1),F(1,0)\in\{F(0,0),F(1,1)\}.}
\tag{C}
\]

This is not arbitrary-source closure and assumes no global spin order.

For the event flip indicators

\[
v_{xy}=1_{\{F(x,y)\ne x\}},
\]

condition `(C)` implies eventwise

\[
\boxed{v_{11}\le v_{00}+v_{10}},
\qquad
\boxed{v_{00}\le v_{01}+v_{11}}.
\tag{I}
\]

Summing against arbitrary nonnegative Poisson rates gives the same inequalities for the generator flip rates.

At

\[
P_h=\left(\frac1{10000},\frac1{100},\frac{9999}{10000}\right),
\]

the ordinary flip-rate vector is

\[
(a,b,1-c,1),
\]

so scalar closure requires

\[
1\le a+(1-c)=\frac1{5000},
\]

which is impossible.

After the alternating/checkerboard relabeling used for Gray's repulsive case, the even and odd transformed rate vectors are

\[
(b,a,1,1-c),
\qquad
(1-c,1,a,b).
\]

Applying the two inequalities in `(I)` gives at either parity

\[
 b\le a+(1-c),
\]

but at `P_h`

\[
\frac1{100}>\frac1{5000}.
\]

Therefore neither the ordinary nor the checkerboard scalar/two-type Gray splice can survive even one boundary event with the exact marginal generator.

No-crossing, permanent coalescence, protected edge pairs and Gray's edge-density argument are therefore moot.
