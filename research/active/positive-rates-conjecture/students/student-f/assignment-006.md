## Why the spine moved

The principal supplied an exact centered predecessor-trail decomposition of the nonempty-exit dual term. In the residual family, with

$$
B=b+c-a,
\qquad
p_*=\frac cB,
\qquad
q_* = \frac{b-a}{B},
\qquad
\omega=1-c+a,
$$

the canonical trail has positive scalar factor `e^{-omega tau}` and selected trail interactions are births. The right region is uniformly bounded after the final coin is averaged before absolute values.

For fixed `R`, finite zero-boundary relaxation then reduces the nonempty-trail term to the all-depth invariant criterion

$$
B(b-a)^{n-1}
\int_{(0,\infty)^n}
 e^{-\omega|u|}
 |\pi^0_{m,r}(F_{x,u})|\,du
\longrightarrow0,
\qquad n=r-x+1\to\infty.
\tag{L}
$$

At exact East, the invariant expectation is structurally zero because the final trail birth inserts a centered character independent of the preceding factor.

A sufficient theorem would be an all-depth centered signed-measure contraction

$$
(b-a)\int_0^\infty e^{-\omega u}
\|\mathcal C_{y,u}\nu\|_{*,y-1}\,du
\le
\theta\|\nu\|_{*,y},
\qquad \theta<1,
\tag{T}
$$

on the class generated by the trail transfers

$$
(\mathcal C_{y,u}\nu)(f)
=
\nu(h_{p_*}(\eta_y)P_u^{<y,0}f).
$$

The norm cannot be total variation on all signed measures.
