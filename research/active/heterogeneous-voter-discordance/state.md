## Setup retained

The intended target was to extend regular-random-graph voter discordance theory to bounded-degree undirected configuration models with unequal degrees. The initial observation was that the conserved voter coordinate is degree-weighted,

$$
B^\pi(\eta)=\sum_x\frac{d_x}{2m}\eta_x,
$$

and its bracket is driven by the degree-weighted discordant-edge sum

$$
\Gamma(B^\pi)(\eta)
=\frac1{4m^2}
\sum_{\{x,y\}\in E:\eta_x\ne\eta_y}(d_x+d_y).
$$
