## What survived the audit

The strongest plausible novelty anchor is the **killed typed patch factorization/representation** for arbitrary finite-state single-site replacement IPS:

- signed typed FK dual;
- successful record hides post-source outcome;
- one-site patch averaging;
- typed target conflicts send the dual to cemetery;
- bare skeleton conditioning fails because cemetery deletes future record constraints;
- multiplying by the noncemetery indicator yields an exact weighted product factorization and hence the bulk/end patch representation.

No direct predecessor for that full interface was found. Closest conceptual predecessors are marked-Poisson clan-of-ancestors constructions and Lubetzky--Sly information percolation; signed FK duality is separately known from Dawson--Greven.

## What did not survive as independent novelty

1. Finite-state typed signed duality: **known ingredients, assembly plausibly new**.
2. `d=3` finite spectral criterion: **known / directly subsumed** by third-order SISO external-positivity theory. In particular Lin--Fang 1997 and Weller--Martin 2020 already give exact third-order nonnegative-impulse/monotone-response characterizations.
3. Exchange-symmetric algebraic criterion: **known ingredients, assembly plausibly new**; useful exact IPS translation, not a primary novelty anchor.
4. Metzler positivity, lumpability, finite Markov spectral calculus: standard.
