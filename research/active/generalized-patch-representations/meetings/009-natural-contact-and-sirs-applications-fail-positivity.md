Krone's two-stage contact process was selected before any typed coefficient calculation from a bounded literature set containing spatial SIRS and Neuhauser's multitype contact process.

## 2. The distinctive killed-patch geometry is genuinely present

For each adult-neighbour target `tau`, the two-stage process has outgoing signed row

\[
(\lambda,-\lambda,-\lambda).
\]

A successful record reveals source type `1` and target type `2` while hiding three post-source outcomes `0,1,2` with signs `+,-,-`.

An incoming target type `2` can meet an existing type-1 active label and send the dual to cemetery. This conflict is realizable through initial data, an earlier hidden outcome `1`, or the empty-target backward retyping generated by maturation.

Hence the Assignment-002 killed/noncemetery repair is not cosmetic in this model.

## 3. Nevertheless bulk positivity fails everywhere in the interacting range

The signed interior transfer is

\[

\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&0\\
0&-(1+\delta+\gamma)&0\\
0&\gamma&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The same-source outgoing-to-outgoing descriptor is realized because hidden outcome `1` has positive absolute mass and the next successful record again requires pre-source type `1`.

Its numerator satisfies

\[
N_{OO}(0)=-\lambda,
\]

and in fact

\[
N_{OO}(t)<0
\]

for every finite `t>=0` whenever `lambda>0`. The killed-reference denominator is strictly positive.

Thus the two-stage contact process is not typed patch positive anywhere in its nontrivial birth regime.

At the exact gate point

\[
\lambda=\gamma=\delta=1,
\qquad e^{-t}=1/2,

the realized bulk patch has

\[

\qquad D_{OO}=5/16,

## 4. The failure is structural, not model-specific

The bounded second candidate, spatial SIRS, gives the same nonempty outgoing row

\[
(\lambda,-\lambda,-\lambda),
\]

now for the infection target. It therefore has the same negative repeated-source `OO` patch throughout its interacting infection range.

This yields a local catalytic-birth no-go:

> if a positive nonempty target mode appears in `0->r` but not in active-source transitions into `r`, then the indicator-basis dual has `a_r^r(tau)<0`; if the source-`r` record can repeat, a realized arbitrarily short `OO` patch is negative.

The no-go explains both applications and substantially narrows the model classes in which typed patch positivity can be expected.

## 5. Existing model theory leaves no hidden positive consequence to claim

Krone already constructed a multitype dual. Foxall simplified the duality proof, resolved most original open questions, and included the two-stage process in a general additive multitype growth theory with complete convergence. Sturm--Swart's general pathwise duality also subsumes Krone's duality.

Therefore no contribution claim is made for ordinary duality, monotonicity, survival theory, or complete convergence.

The killed typed representation is genuinely different as a representation, but the negative bulk patch prevents the patch-positive comparison/convergence mechanism from producing a new theorem for the base model.

The first application block has done its job: it found a hard natural model, specialized the representation exactly, and rejected positivity rather than tuning the model.

A repeat search among catalytic birth/contact/SIRS variants would now be low-value because the no-go lemma decides a broad part of that space in advance.
