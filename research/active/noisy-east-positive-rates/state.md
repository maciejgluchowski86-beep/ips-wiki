## Current obstruction

The principal's latest one-site long-lived-state wall criterion leaves a residual region adjacent to the East boundary. Near that boundary, neither state supplies a one-site common-state wall with enough regeneration margin. Positive noise is expected to destroy the exceptional East behavior, but generic noise also destroys reversibility and the explicit equilibrium structure used by standard East arguments.

The first question is whether the **wall mechanism itself** can be strengthened from one agreed site to the smallest nontrivial agreed block.

## First assignment

Construct the exact two-site agreed-block disagreement/regeneration process under the canonical coupling, with the influencing exterior state treated adversarially.

Define killing as a disagreement crossing the block from the influencing side before the block regenerates to full agreement. Compute the exact killed finite-state generator or next-generation operator and the corresponding crossing/regeneration factor.

Determine:

1. whether the factor is `<1` on any open part of the currently unresolved noisy-East region;
2. whether a uniform `<1` bound can survive as the East boundary is approached with positive noise;
3. if not, the exact obstruction and whether it rules out only length two or the whole finite-block-wall idea.

This is a falsification task before theorem-building. A negative answer that kills the finite-wall extension is useful narrowing.
